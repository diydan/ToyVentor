Recipe v1 fields:
- "v": 1
- "id": lowercase letters, digits and underscores
- "name": 2 to 4 friendly words
- "stat": one of "Speed", "Spin", "Torque", "Glow"
- "behavior": one short sentence

- No brand or trademark words anywhere.
