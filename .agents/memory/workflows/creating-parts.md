# Creating parts

For every request to create or change ToyVentor parts (robots, figurines, add-ons, capsules, bricks, mounts or Pro mechanisms), read and follow `docs/parts/creating-parts.md` before modelling. Resolve the brief and catalog slot first, take the part through the documented PartSpec and assembly gates, and finish with the guide's evidence handoff. Do not describe a part as print-tested, safe or release-ready without the physical evidence described there.
