# ToyVentor brand style

Decided by Dan, 2026-10-29. Every character, robot, animal and accessory follows these six rules.

## The six-point signature

1. **Crown buds.** Every head carries two studs on its crown, centred, in the accent colour. They are the silhouette mark and stay functional (hats, ears and horns fit on them).
2. **Made of pieces.** A waist band at every joint: each part's lower edge steps in (about 35 degrees, printable), so every build reads as stacked parts.
3. **One face language.** Big head; eyes set low and wide; expressions as decals or game skins, never moulded, so a child projects the feeling.
4. **One accent colour.** The spark, `#FF6B4A`, on the crown buds of every character.
5. **One signature motion.** The toy waddle, the hop and the victory spin, the same for every character in the game.
6. **One idea: "I was made."** Every character is proud to have been built: a name, a one-line story and a page in the Toybook, plus earned rare variants.

## Tests every new character must pass

- **Family test:** in solid black, front view, it reads as ToyVentor (head-heavy stack, two crown buds, banded waist).
- **Individual test:** in solid black, it is recognisable on its own: its one defining feature (ears, horns, antenna, hair, tail) is big enough to change the outline.
- **Print test:** every printed part passes the generator and the pre-print checks (no supports, no steep overhangs, no ledges).

## Guardrails

- Borrow principles, never features. No mouthless cat with a bow, no melancholy puffy-eyed kid, no other brand's line style or character shapes. Work from written feature lists, not from another product's images.
- No paid random packs for children. Free daily packs and earned rarities give the collecting thrill without loot-box risk.
- Concept may be close to existing modular-character packs; execution must be our own (printable stud parts, one body standard, this signature).
