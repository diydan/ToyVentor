Principles

Flat glass, raised buttons

Panels are frosted glass: thin rim, one soft shadow. Buttons are the only raised objects, with a solid edge underneath that invites a press.

One sky button

Sky blue marks the single main action, the selected state, and the active tab. Violet is the second action and the Pro tag. The Snap/Pro switch uses sky for selected.

Gold means reward

Gold stays rare: legendary items, big rewards, and the Snap tag. Use it anywhere else and it stops meaning anything.

Rewards get the show

Glow, particles, and sound are saved for pulls and unlocks. Resting screens stay calm.

Glass and depth

Glass panel

  • Fill glass, 1px glass-line rim, radius-lg.
  • One soft drop shadow (shadow-glass). Nothing else — no gloss strip, no inner highlight.
  • Selected: 1px sky border + small sky glow (shadow-glass-selected).

Raised button

  • 56px tall, radius-md, Rubik 700 label.
  • Solid darker edge 5px below (sky-700/violet-700), lit top edge, soft colored shadow.
  • Pressed: sinks 4px as the edge shrinks to 1px, in 80ms.
  • Ghost buttons get a 3–4px edge in line-soft. Disabled is flat. Chips stay flat pills with no edge, so they never read as buttons.
  • Label is always the dark ink color, never white.

Accessibility

  • Text meets 4.5:1 on its surface; controls, focus rings, and bar fills meet 3:1.
  • Every tap target 44px or taller. Buttons are 56px.
  • Color never works alone: rarity has a word and pips, moods have their word, Snap and Pro have their names, checks say OK.
  • Focus ring is 3px gold with a 3px offset on every control.
  • No flashing faster than 3 times a second — glow, Reveal, and intro included.
  • Every sound has a visual twin. The app is fully playable muted.

Needed from design

Brand

  • Logo, wordmark, app icon.
  • Mascot for empty states and the Reveal card.
  • Icon set: rounded, 2.5px stroke, round caps. No emoji.

Art

  • Lit render style for 3D parts on dark, with a rim light.
  • Pack art, character card frames, the five rarity frames.
  • Skin styles; star and glow art for the ground.

Screens still to style

  • Onboarding (5), Toybox, Scan, Part detail, 3D print.
  • Pack Rip, Play, Game Mode, Reveal, More, Mix and match.
  • Empty, error, loading, offline states; the order quote.

Open

  • Day (light) theme: not designed yet.
  • Intro scene needs motion design to the storyboard.
  • Nav labels (Home/Build/More here vs Factory/Packs/Market in the wireframe) need one decision across docs.
