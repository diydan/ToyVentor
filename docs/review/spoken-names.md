# Spoken names: native-speaker review sheet

_Generated by `scripts/spoken-names-sheet.ts` from `src/lib/propNames.ts` and `src/lib/partNames.ts`. Do not edit by hand; edit the vocabulary and regenerate._

When a child taps a prop in the game, or a part in Describe & Build, the game says its colour and name in the chosen language. The phrases
are **built** from a noun (with gender and number) and a colour word that agrees with it, not machine-translated, so a wrong noun or a
wrong colour form is wrong in every phrase that uses it. That makes a review short: check the nouns, the colour forms, and a few built phrases.

## How to review (about 10 minutes per language)

1. Open the section for your language. Read each **bare noun** aloud: is it the word a child there would use for this toy?
2. Read the **colour forms**: do the masculine, feminine and plural forms agree the way you would say them?
3. Read the **built phrases** (every noun in a plain colour, and in an awkward one): any that sounds wrong or stiff?
4. Answer the numbered **doubts** (they are mine, not facts).
5. Tap a few things in the app with the language chosen, and tell us whether the **voice** is in your language and accent (see "Same spelling" below).

Reply with the corrected word next to the old one; that is all we need.

## Same spelling in more than one language (the voice may use the wrong one)

The text-to-speech model is given only the words and decides the language itself. A phrase spelled identically in two languages has no
signal: `casa verde` is valid Spanish, Italian and Portuguese; `luz azul` is Spanish and Portuguese. The voice may answer in the other
language or accent. Counts below are over every noun/part in every colour (not all are used by the game at once).
