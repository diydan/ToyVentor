## 1. Top Bar & Navigation
- **Left dropdown = `Toy Box` (`[mini toy image] Toy Box ▾`):**
  - Label stays **"Toy Box"** (instead of swapping to the toy's text name with a plain color dot).
  - Shows a **mini SVG icon** of the active built-in toy/vehicle, or a **Meta Muse Image**–generated mini toy image for scanned/custom toys.
- **Top-right = Settings (`⚙️`) icon:**
  - **Grown-Ups / Play Time** lives strictly inside the top-right **Settings (`⚙️`)** button.
  - Remove the `Grown-ups · Play Time` button from `Toy Box` completely (`Toy Box` stays 100% kid toys + scan).

## 2. Grown-Up Unlock UX
- Current gate (`1.2s` hold + typed `17 × 8` two-digit multiplication) is clunky and too hard.
- Replace with a **clean, fast 1-step unlock** (tap-only big buttons, no keyboard typing or hard mental math; optional simple 4-tap PIN pad).

## 3. `Toy Box` Roster & Kip / Craft Assets (Early Phase)
- Bring **Kip** and all the modular vehicle/craft assets we already built into `Toy Box`:
  - **Enabled & playable out of the box:**
    1. **Kip (Full)** — full heads, hairstyles, hats, backpacks, hand props, and 3D rig integration.
    2. **One Starter Vehicle** (`Roadster Racer`).
    3. **Scan a Real Toy** (Meta SAM 3.1 + Meta Muse Image icon/skin).
  - **Visible in `Toy Box`, greyed out until unlocked:**
    - Add the rest of our vehicles & modular craft (`Hauler Truck`, `Scooter`, `Motorbike`, `Tractor & Trailer`, `Aeroplane`, `Boat`, `Helicopter`, `Space Shuttle`, `Bolt Mech`, etc.) as greyed-out preview cards so kids see the full lineup they can unlock.

## 4. XP, STEM Accuracy Bonus, Difficulty & Advanced Part Unlocks (Core Phase)
- Add an **XP & Rank progression system** driven by playing and solving STEM levels:
  - **Accuracy bonus on STEM completion:** Every completion earns base XP (no punishment for testing/iterating), and solving with high **accuracy** (e.g. clean 1st/2nd try, exact measurement/balance/ratio) grants bonus XP + mastery stars.
  - **What XP unlocks:**
    - Higher **STEM level difficulty** tiers.
    - The **greyed-out vehicles/craft** in `Toy Box`.
    - **New advanced parts** (`Pro` beams, pins, gears, steering linkages, `Momentum` balloon/rubber-band/hydraulic cores, `Mech` parts).

## 5. Weekly STEM Pack Rips (Late Phase)
- Align **Pack Rips** with:
  1. Toys the kid actually owns/unlocked (every pulled card fits).
  2. **New STEM activities every week** (weekly spotlight lab + matching part drops + bonus XP for trying that week's STEM challenge with your upgraded toy).
