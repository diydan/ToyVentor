## 1. Core Architectural Decisions (Pass 4f Locked)

1. **Hybrid `50 mm` (`4 TU`) Solid-Deck Hull Grid:** `#1 kit_keel_base` (`49.8 × 124.8 × 15.3 mm`, `4×10 TU`) exposes `12.50 mm` `stud_small` top decks alongside `Pro` `bore_grid` (`Ø7.00 mm` free, `5.10 mm` double-D fixed) and `shaft_drive` (`Ø6.60 mm` round, `5.00 mm` flats) running gear, mating with Kip seated legs (`char_legs_seated`), vehicle/racer add-ons, and `Pro` gears without adapters (physical fit pending `F1` calibration). **Zero wire channels are cut into `#1`'s `12.50 mm` deck** (Stage 4 wires run 100% above-deck at `Z_world >= 12.50 mm`, preserving uninterrupted solid roofs above every axle).
2. **Single Universal On-Lattice Axle Tier (`z_axle = 6.25 mm = 0.5 TU`):** The off-lattice `high` axle bore is **deleted**. Every axle station on `#1` (`rows 0, 2, 7, 8`) uses one bottom-entry `45°`-teardrop `c_jaw_tu01` tier at **`z_axle = 6.25 mm`** inside the `12.50 mm` solid deck (`apex = 11.20 mm`, `roof = 1.30 mm >= 1.20 mm`). All land builds (**Race Car, Dump Truck, Farm Tractor**) share bottom-click barbell assembly and the exact **`25.00 mm` (`2.0 TU`)** rear PTO gear center distance (`Z_world = 6.25 mm ↔ 31.25 mm`).
3. **Steered Front Axle at `row 0` (`Y = 6.25 mm`) + Separate `Pro` Bridge:** Positioning the front axle at `row 0` ahead of the `2×3 TU` tapered nose (`rows 0..2`) eliminates tire-to-shoulder clash, giving exact 2D polygon clearances of **`+7.41 mm` shoulder / `+4.17 mm` nose** at **`±26.0°` lock** (`Ø36.0 mm` `#4` wheel, `60.0 cm` wall-to-wall turning circle) and **`+1.97 mm` (`>= 1.80 mm`) shoulder / `+3.32 mm` nose** at **`±21.0°` lock** (`Ø50.0 mm` `#5` wheel, `71.3 cm` wall-to-wall circle). `#1` carries no protruding kingpin ears; Stage 2 (`Pro`) snaps `pro_steer_bridge` onto `rows 0..1` nose studs above the axle jaw.
4. **Exact `10-Row` (`3 + 3 + 4 = 10`) Deck Allocation & Open-Top Cockpit:**
   - `rows 0..2` (`2×3 TU`): Tapered nose + `#7 kit_aero_wedge` (`24.8 mm` wide, clear of steered tire sweep).
   - `rows 3..5` (`4×3 TU`): Open-top `#8 kit_windscreen` (`22.0 mm` tall, zero roof bridge, integral above-deck side-bolster wire ducts) + Kip `2×2` seat (`rows 4..5`), giving unrestricted head-out clearance for the full Kip seated stack (`43.0 × 36.0 × 70.0 mm` to head stud tips above deck).
   - `rows 6..9` (`4×4 TU`): Rear Power & Cargo Bay (`#11 kit_tilting_tub` sized `4×4 TU = 49.8 × 49.8 × 18.0 mm`), with a **`10.0 × 33.0 mm` PTO through-slot** centered on `row 8` (`10` perimeter studs retained).
5. **15 New Rigid Modules (`#3a = 78.0 mm`, `#4/#5 = 12.3 mm` New Kit Wheels) + 3 Smooth `PETG`/`TPU` Flex Skins across 4 `<= 170 × 170 mm` Cards (`A–D`):** Unlocks all 9 canonical craft silhouettes (`60–85%` cross-craft part reuse). Individual parts (`<= 124.8 mm`) accommodate all `[3, 5, 8] mm` slicer brim steps on a `180 × 180 mm` bed; full `<= 170.0 mm` kit plates leave `>= 5.0 mm/side` margin and cap plate-level brims at `<= 5.0 mm` (`<= 180.0 mm` total).

### 2.2 Toolchain & `interfaces.json` Gap Report (Per `AGENTS.md` Rule 3)

Before authoring Pass 5 CAD, the following four connector/socket definitions must be registered in `interfaces.json` and `seat_pair()` / `toyventor_assembly.py` (which currently validates `stud_grid`, `socket_grid`, `bore_grid`, and `pin` in `90°` turns only; arbitrary-angle hinge/steer sweeps run via dedicated Gate `G1` pose scripts):

1. **`socket_small` Reference Geometry (Codifying `toyventor_parts.py` `tube_dims` & `integrated_sockets`):**
   - **`cage` style** (`#7, #8, #11, #14`, Power Cores): outer rim wall `2.00 mm`, design radial stud gap `0.05 mm` (`fit = 0.10 mm`), internal coupling tubes `ID = 6.70 mm` (`ri = 3.35 mm`), `OD = 10.98 mm` (`ro = 5.49 mm`), socket depth `>= 3.20 mm` (`stud_h + 0.40 mm`), closed ceiling roof `>= 1.50 mm` (plates) / `2.00 mm` (bricks), `96` segments (`FIT_SEG`), internal ceiling bridge span `7.26–7.56 mm` per `bridge_span()` (`toyventor_parts.py:2228`).
   - **`holes` style** (`#13` pod pads): blind stud holes `ID = 6.70 mm`, surrounding boss wall `>= 2.00 mm` (`OD >= 10.70 mm`), depth `3.20 mm`, closed ceiling roof `>= 1.20 mm` (`check I7_hole_roof`).
2. **`c_jaw_tu01` (Pinless Compliant `Ø6.60 mm` Shaft / Hinge Jaw):**
   - **Geometry:** `Ø7.00 mm` (`r_in = 3.50 mm`) circular seat with `45°` teardrop crown (`R_fillet = 1.00 mm` at apex), `242.0°` total wrap (`θ_tip = ±121.0°` from top apex) -> **`6.00 mm` throat** (`2 × 3.50 × sin 59.0° = 6.00 mm`; diametral interference `0.60 mm` on `Ø6.60 mm` `shaft_drive`, i.e., **`δ = 0.30 mm/prong >= 0.25 mm`** `min_snap_mm` at `interfaces.json:180`), prong thickness **`t = 1.60 mm`** (`R_m = 4.30 mm`), axial bearing land **`b = 3.00 mm`** per side wall (`I = b t³ / 12 = 1.024 mm⁴`), `30°` entry chamfers.
   - **Reproducible Analytical Estimate (Gated on `G7`, Not Pre-Verified):** Assuming PLA modulus `E = 3,200 MPa`, deck-clamped root half-angle `θ_clamp = 25.0°`, single-prong free arc `Δθ = 121.0° - 25.0° = 96.0°` (`1.6755 rad`, combined `2Δθ = 3.3510 rad`), curved-beam inner-fiber factor `1.3×`, and Castigliano integral `c_int = ∫_{25°}^{121°} (cos θ - cos 121°)² dθ = 1.3177`: estimated inner-fiber strain is `1.3 × t × (2δ) / (2 R_m² × 2Δθ) =` **`1.01%`** (`< 1.80%` PLA yield), radial stiffness `k = E I / (R_m³ c_int) = 31.2 N/mm` -> **`9.4 N/prong`** spreading force (`0.0%` resting strain once seated in `Ø7.00 mm`). Physical acceptance is governed strictly by **Gate `G7`** (`F1` coupon printed in `#1` `z_local = 0` pose: `500` cycles + `24 h @ 40 °C`, post-test pull-out `>= 6.0 N`).
3. **`flank_rail_tu02` (Asymmetric Keyed Wing/Skid Mortise — Geometric Poka-Yoke):**
   - **Geometry:** Rectangular pocket `18.0 mm` (`Y`) × `4.60 mm` (`Z`, `Z_world = 4.00 .. 8.60 mm`, `45°` chamfered ceiling, `8.0 mm` depth `< 20.0 mm` bridge limit), with a **`3.0 × 2.0 mm` forward index step** on the `-Y` edge only and dual `0.25 mm` horizontal friction detents (`fit = 0.12 mm`). Rejects `5.00 mm` `shaft_drive` flats and prevents backward wing/skid insertion.
4. **Rotary Bearing Relief Rule:** Relieved central axle tunnels (`Ø8.20 mm` between `3.00 mm` outer bearing lands) prevent FDM seam/over-extrusion binding across `49.8 mm` hulls; empirical friction is logged during `F1`.
