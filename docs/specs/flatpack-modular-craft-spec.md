## 1. Core Architectural Decisions (Pass 4g Locked)

1. **Hybrid `50 mm` (`4 TU`) Solid-Deck Hull Grid:** `#1 kit_keel_base` (`49.8 × 124.8 × 15.3 mm`, `4×10 TU`) exposes `12.50 mm` `stud_small` top decks alongside `Pro` `bore_grid` (`Ø7.00 mm` free, `5.10 mm` double-D fixed) and `shaft_drive` (`Ø6.60 mm` round, `5.00 mm` flats) running gear, mating with Kip seated legs (`char_legs_seated`), vehicle/racer add-ons, and `Pro` gears without adapters (physical fit pending `F1` calibration). **Zero wire channels are cut into `#1`'s `12.50 mm` deck** (Stage 4 wires run 100% above-deck at `Z_world >= 17.20 mm`, preserving uninterrupted solid roofs above every axle).
2. **Single Universal On-Lattice Axle Tier (`z_axle = 6.25 mm = 0.5 TU`):** The off-lattice `high` axle bore is **deleted**. Every transverse axle station on `#1` (`rows 0, 7, 8`, keeping `row 1` solid around the `Y = 25.00 mm` vertical `free` steering bore) uses one bottom-entry `50°`-teardrop `c_jaw_tu01` tier at **`z_axle = 6.25 mm`** inside the `12.50 mm` solid deck (`apex = 10.82 mm`, maintaining **`1.68 mm >= 1.20 mm`** solid deck roof full-width). All land builds (**Race Car, Dump Truck, Farm Tractor**) share bottom-click barbell assembly and the exact **`25.00 mm` (`2.0 TU`)** rear PTO gear center distance (`Z_world = 6.25 mm ↔ 31.25 mm`).
3. **Steered Front Axle at `row 0` (`Y = 6.25 mm`) + Inter-Row `(0, 25.00 mm)` Steering Stem + T-Planform `Pro` Bridge:** Positioning the front axle at `row 0` ahead of the `2×3 TU` tapered nose (`rows 0..2`) and placing `#1`'s vertical `Ø7.00 mm` `free` steering bore at the **inter-row lattice center `(X = 0.00, Y = 25.00 mm)`** (`2.0 TU`, `8.84 mm` from all `4` `rows 1..2` studs -> `+2.04 mm` clear to every stud, enclosed inside a solid `row 1..2` nose block) eliminates stud gouging and tire clash. With `pro_steer_bridge` (`48.1 × 24.8 × 15.3 mm` T-planform on `rows 0..1`, rear `2×1` servo studs + smooth front `Z_world = 25.00 mm` rack slideway) and `15.00 mm`-scrub knuckles (`W_track = 67.50 mm`, outer width `79.80 mm`), true 3D surface clearances are **`+6.44 mm` keel / `+3.56 mm` bridge / `+7.23 mm` rack** at **`±26.0°` lock** (`Ø36.0 mm` `#4`, `56.8 cm` wall-to-wall turning circle) and **`+2.05 mm` (`>= 1.80 mm`) keel / `+3.56 mm` bridge / `+7.56 mm` rack** at **`±21.0°` lock** (`Ø50.0 mm` `#5`, `67.3 cm` wall-to-wall circle).
4. **Exact `10-Row` (`3 + 3 + 4 = 10`) Deck Allocation & Open-Top Cockpit:**
   - `rows 0..2` (`2×3 TU`): Tapered nose (`24.8 mm` wide, all `6` studs intact) + `#7 kit_aero_wedge` (`24.8 × 37.3 × 33.25 mm`, clear of steered tire sweep).
   - `rows 3..5` (`4×3 TU`): Open-top `#8 kit_windscreen` (`49.8 × 37.3 × 23.5 mm`, zero roof bridge, `12.3 mm`-wide `cols 0, 3` side bolsters with open-top wire troughs above the `4.70 mm` socket ceiling + `45°` dashboard footwell toe-kick recess clearing seated Kip's toes) + Kip `2×2` seat (`rows 4..5`, `25.20 mm` clear inner leg/torso width `> 24.80 mm`), giving unrestricted head-out clearance for the live Kip seated stack (`48.20 × 41.11 × 80.60 mm` above seat, `Z_world_top = 93.10 mm`).
   - `rows 6..9` (`4×4 TU`): Rear Power & Cargo Bay (`#11 kit_tilting_tub` sized `4×4 TU = 49.8 × 49.8 × 18.0 mm`), with a **`10.0 × 31.4 mm` PTO through-slot** centered on `row 8` (`2.95 mm` rear cross-tie, `10` perimeter studs retained).
5. **15 New Rigid Modules (`#3a = 78.0 mm`, `#3b = 65.0 mm` w/ `28.0 mm` Stem, `#4/#5 = 12.3 mm` Kit Wheels) + 3 Smooth `0.80 mm` `PETG`/`TPU` Flex Skins (`0.60 mm` Fold Corridors) across 4 `<= 170 × 170 mm` Cards (`A–D`):** Unlocks all 9 canonical craft silhouettes (`60–85%` cross-craft part reuse). Individual parts (`<= 124.8 mm`) accommodate all `[3, 5, 8] mm` slicer brim steps on a `180 × 180 mm` bed (`fdm_pla_0.4.json`); full `<= 170.0 mm` kit plates use `fdm_kitplate_0.4.json` (`brim_steps_mm = [3, 5]`, `<= 180.0 mm` total).

### 2.2 Toolchain & `interfaces.json` Registration Specification (Per `AGENTS.md` Rule 3)

1. **`socket_small` Reference Geometry (Codifying `toyventor_parts.py` `tube_dims` & `integrated_sockets`):**
   - **`cage` style** (`#7, #11, #14`, Power Cores): outer rim wall `2.00 mm`, design radial stud gap `0.05 mm` (`fit = 0.10 mm`), internal coupling tubes `ID = 6.70 mm` (`ri = 3.35 mm`), `OD = 10.98 mm` (`ro = 5.49 mm`), socket depth `>= 3.20 mm` (`stud_h + 0.40 mm`), closed ceiling roof `>= 1.50 mm` (plates) / `2.00 mm` (bricks), `96` segments (`FIT_SEG`), internal ceiling bridge span `7.26–7.56 mm` per `bridge_span()`.
   - **`holes` style** (`#8` side bolsters, `#13` pod pads, `pro_steer_bridge`): blind stud holes `ID = 6.70 mm`, surrounding boss wall `>= 2.00 mm` (`OD >= 10.70 mm`), depth `3.20 mm`, closed ceiling roof `>= 1.50 mm` (`check I7_hole_roof`).
2. **`c_jaw_tu01` (Pinless Compliant `Ø6.60 mm` Shaft / Hinge Jaw):**
   - **Geometry:** `Ø7.00 mm` (`r_in = 3.50 mm`) circular seat with `50°` teardrop crown (`apex = z_axle + 4.57 mm`, matching `toyventor_pro.py` `TEARDROP_ROOF_DEG = 50°`), `242.0°` total wrap (`θ_tip = ±121.0°` from top apex) -> **`6.00 mm` throat** (`2 × 3.50 × sin 59.0° = 6.00 mm`; diametral interference `0.60 mm` on `Ø6.60 mm` `shaft_drive`, i.e., **`δ = 0.30 mm/prong >= 0.25 mm`** `min_snap_mm`), prong thickness **`t = 1.60 mm`** (`R_m = 4.30 mm`), axial bearing land **`b = 3.00 mm`** per side wall (`I = b t³ / 12 = 1.024 mm⁴`), `30°` entry chamfers.
   - **Reproducible Analytical Estimate (Gated on `G7`, Not Pre-Verified):** Assuming PLA modulus `E = 3,200 MPa`, deck-clamped root half-angle `θ_clamp = 25.0°`, single-prong free arc `Δθ = 96.0°` (`1.6755 rad`), curved-beam inner-fiber factor `1.3×`, and Castigliano integral `c_int = 1.3176`: estimated inner-fiber strain is **`1.01%`** (`< 1.80%` PLA yield), radial stiffness `k = 31.3 N/mm` -> **`9.4 N/prong`** spreading force (`0.0%` resting strain once seated in `Ø7.00 mm`). Physical acceptance is governed strictly by **Gate `G7`** (`F1` coupon printed in `#1` `z_local = 0` pose: `500` cycles + `24 h @ 40 °C`, post-test pull-out `>= 6.0 N`).
3. **`flank_rail_tu02` (Asymmetric Keyed Wing/Skid Mortise — Geometric Poka-Yoke):**
   - **Geometry:** Rectangular pocket `18.0 mm` (`Y`) × `4.60 mm` (`Z`, `Z_world = 4.00 .. 8.60 mm`, `45°` chamfered ceiling, `8.0 mm` depth `< 20.0 mm` bridge limit), with a **`3.0 × 2.0 mm` forward index step** on the `-Y` edge only and dual `0.25 mm` horizontal friction detents (`fit = 0.12 mm`). Rejects `5.00 mm` `shaft_drive` flats and prevents backward wing/skid insertion.
4. **Rotary Bearing Relief & Full-Width Roof Rule:** Relieved central axle tunnels (`Ø8.20 mm` between `3.00 mm` outer bearing lands) prevent FDM seam binding across `49.8 mm` hulls and are truncated by a **flat horizontal `<= 4.5 mm` bridged crown at `Z_world = 10.82 mm`** so `>= 1.68 mm` solid deck roof holds full-width (`I7_hole_roof` / `V4`).
