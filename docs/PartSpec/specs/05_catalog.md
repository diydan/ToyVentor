- **W5:** 4 parts: two wheels, a hinge, a cross connector.

## How parts were picked

- Covers every connection type and size class, so the standard is exercised end to end.
- Useful on a scanned toy first: add-ons, capsules and mounts weigh more than plain bricks, because that is the product.
- Prints without supports and passes the fit gate; a part that cannot is replaced, not forced.
- Reused by other parts and by the AI design loop; family-built so each new variant is one catalog row.
- Original designs only. No third-party names.

**Rules:**

- Snap grids are at least 2x2: a 1xN part has no tubes, so nothing holds its studs.
- Skins are game-only and are not counted.
- Every Large part must clear the small-parts cylinder.
